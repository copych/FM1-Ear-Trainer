Start playing

On the FM-1: stop playback, hold HOME and press PLAY. Choose Pitch training, Metronome or Rhythm game with SELECT (or K1), then press PLAY. HOME returns to the shared training menu; HOME again leaves training. All activities are part of one Ear Trainer firmware.

Repeat the hidden notes

Choose a single octave or the full F3–G5 keyboard in K2. Each sequence contains distinct pitches. Octave mode uses C4–C5 in major or A3–A4 in minor; full mode extends above and below the reference.

Listen to the full exercise, then play the hidden pitches in any order, separately or together. Each pitch counts once. Highlighted references and notes you have already uncovered can be played again without an error; the game waits for the remaining notes. Pitches include octave.

Display

Four top zones correspond to K1–K4. Note cards show references and hide target pitches; accepted answers turn green, and a failed card is marked red. Correct answers reveal the completed sequence. RATING is your current skill rating; BEST is the saved highest rating for this mode. The bottom is dedicated to a larger patch name.

Training knobs

  • K1 Scale: C major or A natural minor (reference A3).
  • K2 Complexity: hidden notes of total notes: 1 of 1, 1 of 2, 2 of 2, 1 of 3, 2 of 3, 3 of 3, 1 of 4, 2 of 4, 3 of 4, 4 of 4. The ten combinations repeat in two blocks: OCT (one octave), then FULL (whole keyboard). Default: 1 of 2 OCT. When all notes are hidden, the first pitch changes each question.
  • PRESETS knob: select among 24 FM6 sounds followed by 200 Prophet factory programs. Every preset uses poly mode. Changing sound stops playback and replays the same question; after a correct answer it advances to the next question.
  • K4 Time: 0–1000 ms between note starts, in 25 ms steps; default 250 ms. Zero plays a simultaneous chord.
  • K3: reserved.

Preferences (scale, complexity/range, spacing and preset) save automatically after exiting the activity. No flash save interrupts an open training activity. Wait for SAVING SETTINGS to finish before powering off. The emulator remembers these preferences in this browser. Current and best ratings are retained for each scale × complexity × range combination. Hardware saves preferences and progress on exit; leave the activity and wait for saving to finish before switching off. Unsaved session changes are lost if power is cut before exit. Adaptive practice weights remain session-only.

Notes sustain together as an arpeggiated chord, releasing one second after the last note starts. Known notes turn green with an underline while held, and their keyboard keys light up. Notes to guess never light a keyboard key during playback. Changing scale, complexity or spacing starts a fresh question. In the emulator, use the corresponding printed knobs on the panel.

In the browser

For panel button combinations, click Hold HOME, then another button. Or Shift+click any panel button to hold it. A blue outline marks the hold. The next button/key release clears it; Escape or Release held controls releases everything.

  1. Open the emulator and press Start training to enable sound and open the activity menu. Turn SELECT to choose an activity and press PLAY.
  2. Listen, then click a key, use the computer-keyboard note shortcuts, or select an external MIDI keyboard.
  3. Use Replay / Retry or Next question.

On the FM-1

With transport stopped, hold HOME and tap PLAY. PLAY retries; REC generates the next question; HOME returns to the training menu. The built-in keys use a fixed F3–G5 layout in this mode. Release held keys before answering. Matching includes octave.

Answer feedback

Green: correct. After one second, the next exercise starts automatically. Red: wrong. Musical keys are blocked, then after one second the same sequence plays again for another attempt. Release held keys before answering. PLAY replays manually; REC skips to the next exercise.

Scores and practice

Ratings start at zero and never go below zero. A first-attempt answer without replay raises the rating. A mistake lowers it; further failed attempts also lower it. Solving after a mistake gives no additional gain. A manual replay penalizes the question once and removes its clean-answer gain; automatic replays have no additional penalty. Skipping or exiting an unscored question counts as a failure. Gains get smaller and losses larger as the rating rises. Ratings range from 0 to 2000. There is no speed bonus. Compare ratings within the same mode; sound and spacing do not create separate tables.

Exercises cover degrees 2–8 of the selected scale, including the seventh. Missing a degree increases its chance in future exercises, up to four times its initial weight. A first-attempt answer without replay reduces that extra weight. Every degree remains possible. Practice weights reset on restart; current and best ratings are saved.

Before installation

Open the backup editor and preserve your sounds and projects. The installer uses this project's local firmware and paired resource image and retains Melodee’s update checks. The optional bootloader step changes the device's bootloader; read the installer explanation before choosing it.

Entering pitch training loads the selected training sound into track 1 of the in-memory project. Other parts are temporarily muted; track 1 retains the selected sound on exit. Do not save over a project whose previous sound you want to keep.

This is an unverified hardware prototype. Both known flash IDs pass software checks, but physical revision compatibility, audio deadlines and recovery remain to be tested. No device is written merely by opening these pages.

Metronome and rhythm games

Open Ear Trainer with HOME + PLAY (or Start training in the browser), choose Metronome or Rhythm game, and press PLAY. HOME returns to the menu so you can switch activities; HOME again exits. Outside training, HOME + REC remains a direct rhythm shortcut; HOME exits that shortcut directly.

PLAY starts/stops. REC taps quarter-note tempo: the first tap starts measurement, then up to four intervals are averaged. A gap over two seconds starts a new measurement. BPM is always quarter notes, including in eighth- and sixteenth-note meters. For example, 6/8 at 60 BPM has six eighth-note clicks every three seconds.

There is one settings page. Volume is always 100%, subdivision 1 and the matching window ±60 ms, capped below half the spacing between required taps at fast tempos.

  • K1: tempo, 30–300 BPM.
  • K2: meter/grouping, including 4/4 eighths (4/4e), triplets (4/4t) and sixteenths (4/4s).
  • K3: game mode: Full bars, Odds or Evens.
  • K4: audible/hidden bars: 1/1 or 2/2.
  • PRESETS: 16 factory setups followed by USER 1–4. Select a user slot and press OCT+ to save. Empty slots leave settings unchanged. Presets keep you in the current activity. They save tempo, meter, game mode, bar cycle and timing correction.
  • OCT-: restart from the first audible guide bar. ALGORITHM: input timing correction −100…+100 ms. Factory presets retain your correction; user slots save their own.

Available meters/groupings: 4/4, 2/4, 3/4, 6/8 (3+3), 9/8 (3+3+3), 12/8 (3+3+3+3), 5/8 (2+3 or 3+2), 7/8 (2+2+3, 3+2+2 or 2+3+2), 5/4 (3+2), 7/4 (4+3), 11/8 (3+3+3+2), 13/8 (3+3+3+2+2), 15/8 (3+3+3+3+3), 3/2, 7/16 (2+2+3), and 1/4. The three extra 4/4 pulse grids retain a four-quarter-note bar. Group starts have stronger clicks.

Play the rhythm game

Start immediately with the first audible guide bar, with no extra precount or preparation pause. Each round is eight bars. With 1/1, bars 1, 3, 5 and 7 are audible guides; bars 2, 4, 6 and 8 are hidden. With 2/2, bars 1–2 and 5–6 are guides; bars 3–4 and 7–8 are hidden.

Tap only missing pulses in hidden bars: Full bars silences every pulse; Odds silences 1, 3, 5…; Evens silences 2, 4, 6…. Remaining pulses stay audible. Numbering restarts each bar, including in odd meters and subdivided grids. Evens requires at least two pulses, so choosing it in 1/4 switches to 2/4. Guide bars always play every pulse; tapping them counts as extra.

Use any built-in keyboard key or MIDI note; release between taps. Simultaneous local keys submit one tap. The header shows LISTEN in audible bars and TAP SILENCE in hidden bars. Pulse visuals appear only in audible bars; hidden bars have no moving beat/bar hint. Metronome plays the complete selected grid continuously without scoring. A soft-edged circle shrinks and fades after feedback: green for matched taps, red for misses or extra taps (green is neutral feedback in free practice). It does not pulse automatically on hidden beats.

Read your timing results

SCORE ranges from 0–100%. Each matched tap receives less credit as its error approaches the tolerance limit. Misses and extra taps reduce the score; duplicate taps cannot earn another hit. At fast tempos the matching window is capped below half the closest spacing between required taps so targets cannot overlap. ERROR is the mean absolute error among matched taps; BIAS is the signed mean (negative early, positive late); SD measures consistency in milliseconds. Low error and low SD are better. A round with no taps scores zero; zero displayed error with zero hits does not mean accurate playing. Misses and extras appear in the result. Results stay visible until PLAY/OCT- or a setting change starts another round.

Input/output latency affects measured timing. Prefer wired audio. If repeated rounds show a consistent +25 ms bias, turn ALGORITHM to +25 ms; a positive correction subtracts delay from incoming taps. A negative correction shifts early input later. In the browser, captured tap times are aligned automatically with the audio output clock when supported, with a latency estimate as fallback. Start ALGORITHM at 0 ms; use it only for a repeatable input bias that remains after this correction. This is not a physical latency measurement and cannot remove unreported Bluetooth, USB or human jitter. Recheck when changing devices. Player taps produce a distinct lower click, including extra taps. Audio is never fed into the judge: only keyboard and MIDI edges count. Guide clicks are louder, and still follow MASTER.

Settings and four user presets are saved on the FM-1 after exiting the metronome screen, avoiding flash interruptions during practice. Let saving finish before switching off. The browser remembers them locally. Results are session-only. Metronome practice temporarily mutes tracks and restores their mute/selection/record state on exit without loading a new instrument sound.

Musical keys remain dimly lit throughout training, with brighter accepted/held-key feedback. The global LIGHTS setting controls the dim level; LIGHTS OFF still disables idle glow. All keys glow equally, so hidden pitches are not revealed.
