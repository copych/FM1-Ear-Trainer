Repeat the hidden notes

Choose a single octave or the full F3–G5 keyboard in K2. Each sequence contains distinct pitches. Octave mode uses C4–C5 in major or A3–A4 in minor; full mode extends above and below the reference.

Listen to the full exercise, then play the hidden ending notes in order. Release keys between answers. Pitches include octave.

Display

Four top zones correspond to K1–K4. Note cards show references and hide target pitches; accepted answers turn green, and a failed card is marked red. Correct answers reveal the completed sequence. SCORE is the session point total; STREAK counts consecutive first-attempt successes without replay. The bottom is dedicated to a larger patch name.

Training knobs

  • K1 Scale: C major or A natural minor (reference A3).
  • K2 Complexity: hidden notes of total notes: 1 of 1, 1 of 2, 2 of 2, 1 of 3, 2 of 3, 3 of 3, 1 of 4, 2 of 4, 3 of 4, 4 of 4. The ten combinations repeat in two blocks: OCT (one octave), then FULL (whole keyboard). Default: 1 of 2 OCT. When all notes are hidden, the first pitch changes each question.
  • PRESETS knob: select among 24 FM6 sounds followed by 200 Prophet factory programs. Every preset uses poly mode. Changing sound stops playback and replays the same question; after a correct answer it advances to the next question.
  • K4 Time: 0–1000 ms between note starts, in 25 ms steps; default 250 ms. Zero plays a simultaneous chord.
  • K3: reserved.

Preferences (scale, complexity/range, spacing and preset) save automatically during quiet answer pauses or after exiting training. Wait for SAVING SETTINGS to finish before powering off. The emulator remembers these preferences in this browser. Scores and practice history reset on restart.

Notes sustain together as an arpeggiated chord, releasing one second after the last note starts. Known notes turn green with an underline while held, and their keyboard keys light up. Notes to guess never light a keyboard key during playback. Changing scale, complexity or spacing starts a fresh question. In the emulator, use the corresponding printed knobs on the panel.

In the browser

For panel button combinations, click Hold HOME, then another button. Or Shift+click any panel button to hold it. A blue outline marks the hold. The next button/key release clears it; Escape or Release held controls releases everything.

  1. Open the emulator and press Switch on to enable sound.
  2. Press Start training.
  3. Listen, then click a key, use the computer-keyboard note shortcuts, or select an external MIDI keyboard.
  4. Use Replay / Retry or Next question.

On the FM-1

With transport stopped, hold HOME and tap PLAY. PLAY retries; REC generates the next question; HOME exits. The built-in keys use a fixed F3–G5 layout in this mode. Release held keys before answering. Matching includes octave.

Answer feedback

Green: correct. After one second, the next exercise starts automatically. Red: wrong. Musical keys are blocked, then after one second the same sequence plays again for another attempt. Release held keys before answering. PLAY replays manually; REC skips to the next exercise.

Scores and practice

Correct answers earn 100, 70, 40 or 10 points on attempts 1–4, then zero. Each manual replay subtracts 10 points, with a minimum of zero; automatic repeats have no extra penalty. PTS shows the session total; S shows consecutive first-attempt answers without manual replay. Skipping breaks the streak. There is no speed bonus.

Exercises cover degrees 2–8 of the selected scale, including the seventh. Missing a degree increases its chance in future exercises, up to four times its initial weight. A first-attempt answer without replay reduces that extra weight. Every degree remains possible. Scores and practice weights are kept until restart or power-off.

Before installation

Open the backup editor and preserve your sounds and projects. The installer uses this project's local firmware and paired resource image and retains Melodee’s update checks. The optional bootloader step changes the device's bootloader; read the installer explanation before choosing it.

Entering training loads TINE EP into track 1 of the in-memory project. Other parts are temporarily muted; track 1 retains TINE EP on exit. Do not save over a project whose previous sound you want to keep.

This is an unverified hardware prototype. Both known flash IDs pass software checks, but physical revision compatibility, audio deadlines and recovery remain to be tested. No device is written merely by opening these pages.
